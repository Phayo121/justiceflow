RIGHTS MUST COME FIRST

Constitutional safeguards

Case administration must respect the Constitution and applicable law. These are high-level signposts, not legal advice or a complete statement of rights.

16

Personal liberty · Section 16

Arrest and detention are subject to constitutional safeguards, including information about reasons and access to legal representation.

18

Dignity · Section 18

Every person's dignity is inviolable; the Constitution prohibits torture and inhuman or degrading treatment or punishment.

20

Equality before the law · Section 20

All persons are equal before and under the law and are entitled to equal protection of the law.

21

Fair hearing · Section 21

A person facing a criminal charge is entitled to a fair hearing and related safeguards, including the presumption of innocence.

22

Search and communications · Section 22

The Constitution addresses searches of persons and property, entry onto premises, and searches of private communications.

Constitution referenceConstitution of the Kingdom of Swaziland Act, 2005 · Chapter III, especially Sections 16, 18, 20, 21 and 22
